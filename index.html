Nepal


Nepal is a landlocked country in South Asia, primarily located within the Himalayas. It borders China to the north and India to the south, east, and west. Nepal is renowned for its diverse geography, including fertile plains, forested hills, and eight of the world's ten highest mountains, including Mount Everest. The capital city is Kathmandu.
The Gorkha Kingdom unified Nepal in the 18th century, and the Shah dynasty established the Kingdom
Kathmandu, Pokhara, Lalitpur, Biratnagar, Bharatpur, and Birgunj
Nepal's culture is a rich tapestry woven from religious traditions, ethnic diversity, and unique customs
Nepal's tourism sector is a significant part of the country's economy, drawing in tourists for various experiences like trekking, adventure activities, and cultural exploration.
