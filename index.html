Nepal is a landlocked country in South Asia, primarily located within the Himalayas. It borders China to the north and India to the south, east, and west. Nepal is renowned for its diverse geography, including fertile plains, forested hills, and eight of the world's ten highest mountains, including Mount Everest. The capital city is Kathmandu.
